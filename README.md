# Bank-Marketing-Campaign-Performance-Analysis
![Main Dashboard](image.png)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes the performance of a bank marketing campaign aimed at promoting term deposit subscriptions to customers.
The analysis focuses on:
- Identifying customer segments with the highest conversion rate
- Evaluating campaign effectiveness
- Understanding factors influencing successful subscription
- Providing business recommendations to improve future marketing campaigns

## Dataset
- Source: Kaggle (https://www.google.com/url?q=https%3A%2F%2Farchive.ics.uci.edu%2Fml%2Fdatasets%2Fbank%2Bmarketing)
- Total records: 41.188
- Features: 21 columns
- Industry: Cosmetics & Beauty Startup

## Business Problem
The bank conducted direct marketing campaigns through phone calls to promote term deposit subscriptions. However, not all contacted customers successfully subscribed.This project aims to answer:
- Which customer segments have the highest conversion rate?
- What factors influence subscription success?
- Which communication channels and campaign strategies are most effective?
- How can the bank improve future campaign performance?

## 🧪 Analytical Methods
- Conversion Rate Analysis
- Crosstab Analysis
- Chi-Square Test of Independence
